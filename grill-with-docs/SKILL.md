name: grill-with-docs
description: Use when a plan or design needs sharpening AND the decisions should leave durable artifacts behind - a relentless interview that writes ADRs and a glossary as it goes.
---

Run a `/grilling` session, using the `/domain-modeling` skill.

The initiative here is the user's. Start one when they ask, or when a design
discussion has produced decisions worth recording — not in the middle of
unrelated work. An unwanted round of questions costs the user a turn and
derails what they were actually doing.

Prefer this over `/grill-me` when the answers should outlive the conversation.
